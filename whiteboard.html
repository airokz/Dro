A space for quick ideas

Draw a shape, then double-click it to add text. Drag an arrow between shapes; it will stay connected when they move. Select an arrow and drag either round endpoint anywhere along a shape edge to reconnect it. The attachment stays in place when the shape moves or resizes. Drag the line itself to detach and move the whole arrow.

Add images using the toolbar, drag and drop, or ⌘ / Ctrl V. Drag a corner to resize. Image proportions are preserved; hold Shift to resize freely.

V select · H pan · R rectangle · O ellipse · D diamond · A arrow · T text · I image. Hold Shift while drawing a rectangle, ellipse, or diamond for a perfect square or circle, or while resizing one to keep its proportions.

⌘ / Ctrl A select all · ⌘ / Ctrl Z undo · Delete delete · Esc deselect. ⌘ / Ctrl S saves the file.

The .json file contains your diagram and all its images. Browser autosave is an extra copy; save important work to a file. No data is sent to the internet.
